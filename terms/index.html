Service terms and privacy

Repairs, diagnostics and upgrades

Tell us the fault and any previous repairs. The shop will confirm the proposed work, estimated cost in INR and expected turnaround after checking the device. Ask about any diagnostic charge before leaving the device. If a new fault or extra work comes up, confirm the revised quote before approving it.

Back up important files before drop-off where possible. Tell us if the device contains data you need to preserve and discuss migration or recovery before approving storage replacement, formatting or an operating system reinstall.

Purchases, warranties and returns

Confirm the exact item, compatibility, price and warranty coverage before you buy. Coverage, return eligibility and any cancellation charges depend on the item, supplier and agreed terms. Keep your invoice and contact the shop with the item details if something goes wrong.

Gaming requests, cancellations and refunds

The booking form sends a request through WhatsApp. Your slot is reserved once Wildcat confirms it. Check the game, platform, start time, duration and price before visiting. To change or cancel a slot, contact the shop with your confirmed booking details.

For any advance payment, confirm the cancellation and refund terms before paying. If you need help with an existing payment, share your booking or receipt reference with the shop.

Privacy and contact details

We use the information you share to respond to enquiries, handle repairs and purchases, or follow up on booking requests. The WhatsApp form opens a message for you to review and send, and WhatsApp handles that message through its service. Keep passwords, card details and verification codes out of booking messages.

For questions about your information or a device left for service, email Wildcat.
