Tell us what you’re seeing

If the laptop won’t start, shuts down during use, runs hot or keeps losing charge, tell us when the problem began and whether it happens on battery, on the charger or both.

Make an older laptop feel faster

An SSD upgrade can improve boot times, app launches and file loading. We check your exact model first. Laptops with an NVMe-capable M.2 slot can use an NVMe SSD. Older models with a compatible 2.5-inch SATA bay may benefit from a SATA SSD.

Tell us your laptop model and how much storage you need. Ask about moving your files before replacing the drive.

What to bring

Bring your laptop and charger if the problem involves power or charging. Bring a photo of any error message you can reproduce, and back up important files if the laptop still boots.

Before approving work

Ask the shop about diagnosis, parts availability, turnaround and the quote. If repair is uneconomical, ask about our current second-hand laptop stock.
